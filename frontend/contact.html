Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common travel, transportation, and ticketing questions.

How far is Lumbini from Bhairahawa, and what is the best transport?

Lumbini is located approximately 27 km west of Bhairahawa center. The drive takes around 35–45 minutes. For maximum comfort with luggage or family, our AC Car Rental is recommended. For local exploration within the sacred garden zone, zero-emission E-Rickshaws or rental bicycles are the most popular choices.

How does dynamic transport pricing work on CityVerse?

Our pricing is completely transparent and based on a fixed base fare plus actual destination distance in kilometers. For example, Bike Rentals have a NPR 500 base + NPR 5/km rate, while Car Rentals are NPR 3,000 base + NPR 20/km. You can toggle one-way or round-trip on our Bookings page to view the exact live fare breakdown before confirming.

Can I book a vehicle as a guest without creating an account?

Yes! EasyTravels supports instant guest reservations. You can enter your contact phone and email to receive an instant digital boarding pass and QR code with zero account creation required.

What are the opening times and ticket fees for Maya Devi Temple?

The Maya Devi Sacred Garden is open daily from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Entry fees are NPR 20 for Nepali citizens, NPR 100 for SAARC nationals, and NPR 200 for international visitors. Meditation grounds and the monastic zone remain accessible during daylight hours.

How do I cancel or reschedule my transport booking?

You can view all your active trips on the Bookings page under the "My Bookings" section. Simply click the "Cancel" button on any active booking to cancel with zero penalty.
