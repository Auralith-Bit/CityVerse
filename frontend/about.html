Historical Journey

Lumbini Across the Millennia

A brief chronicle of the holy grounds from ancient peace to modern discovery.

623 BCE

Birth of Siddhartha Gautama

Queen Maya Devi gave birth to Prince Siddhartha under a Sal tree in Lumbini garden.

249 BCE

Emperor Ashoka's Pilgrimage

Mauryan Emperor Ashoka visited Lumbini and erected the inscribed sandstone pillar marking Buddha's birthplace.

1896 CE

Rediscovery of Ashoka Pillar

General Khadga Shumsher and archaeologist Dr. Alois Führer rediscovered the pillar buried in dense Terai forest.

1997 CE

UNESCO World Heritage Status

Lumbini was officially inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site under international preservation.

2022 CE

Gautam Buddha International Airport

Commercial operations opened at Bhairahawa's international airport, creating direct global air links to Lumbini.

Visitor Guidelines

Sacred Etiquette & Travel Tips

Help preserve the serenity of Lumbini by observing these traditional customs.

Modest Dress Code

Please cover shoulders and knees when visiting the Maya Devi Temple and all monastic compounds.

Footwear Removal

Shoes must be removed before entering the central sanctum of the Maya Devi Temple and meditation halls.

Photography Rules

Photography is allowed in the gardens, but prohibited directly inside the inner Marker Stone chamber.

Weather & Hydration

Carry a water bottle and sun hat. Winter visits (Nov–Feb) are crisp, while summer requires sun protection.
